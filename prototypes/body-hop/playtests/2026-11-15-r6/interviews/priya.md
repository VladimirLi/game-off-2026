**What the game is about:** A puzzle game where you are a white spirit circle. You hop into sleeping bodies (a guard and a cat) and use their abilities to reach EXIT.

**Rules as I understood them**
- Walk into a body to hop into it. Confirmed.
- The guard can pass locked doors. Confirmed.
- The cat passes vents and red blocks, which show as dashed outlines. It walks on blue bridges. Confirmed.
- The spirit and guard treat red blocks as walls. The guard treats blue bridges as pits. Confirmed.
- Plates hold gates open only while a body is on them. The spirit weighs nothing. Confirmed.
