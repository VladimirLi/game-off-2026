AI persona: ruth

Ruth, 61, non-gamer. I played levels 1 and 2 and stopped, a short session.

What the game is about: you are a white circle (a spirit) who can walk into sleeping creatures and take over their bodies. Each body sees different colours and can pass different things. The aim is to reach EXIT.

Rules as I understood them:
- Walking into a guard or cat hops you into it. Confirmed (level 1 message "You are the guard now").
- Guards carry the key and can pass locked doors. Confirmed.
- The spirit sees red and blue blocks as fog, which acts as a wall. Confirmed by the legend. I did not test walking into one.
- The cat sees red blocks only as dashed outlines, so it can walk through them. Confirmed in level 2.
- Pits block the spirit, and I assumed they block the guard and cat too. Guessed, because I routed around them.
- Par is a turn target. Confirmed, I hit par on both levels.
- Hopping costs a turn, as one step into the body. Guessed, but my counts matched par.

Best moment: level 2. I worked out the whole route on my own. The guard goes through the door, hops the cat, and the cat slips through the dashed red to the exit. The turn counter matched par exactly.

Worst moment: the legend panel covers the board when you open it, and the legend scrolls. I had to press L twice to get a clear look at the level. Not bad, just a small surprise.

Where I got stuck: nowhere.

Difficulty: level 1 was 1 of 5, level 2 was 2 of 5.
Satisfaction: level 1 was 4 of 5, level 2 was 5 of 5.

Would I play on? Yes, probably, in short sessions. The messages are clear and I like that the rules are taught one at a time.
