Game jam judge, about 15 minutes per game. I played levels 1-7 in 10 tool calls and hit par on every one.

What the game is about: a small puzzle game. You start as a white spirit and hop into sleeping bodies (guard, cat). Each body sees different things. You get to the exit by chaining bodies.

Rules as I understood them:
- Walking into a body hops you into it. Confirmed.
- Guards carry the key, so a guard can pass locked doors. Confirmed.
- Red blocks are walls to the guard and to the spirit. The cat walks through them, since they are only dashed outlines to it. Confirmed.
- The blue bridge only exists for the cat. Confirmed.
- You can hop between adjacent bodies even if one is inside a wall or a doorway. Confirmed in level 4.
- A plate holds a gate open only while a body stands on it. The spirit weighs nothing. The body you leave behind keeps standing there, so it keeps the plate down. Confirmed.
- Only a cat fits through a vent. Confirmed.
- Par is the turn count. Guessed it counts every step and hop; it matched my planned counts exactly.

Best moment: level 6, "Step aside". The cat steps into the wall so the guard can pass, and you get back into the guard by hopping sideways from inside the wall. It felt clever.

Worst moment: none really. Level 1's par message was a bit anticlimactic because the level is trivial.

Where I got stuck: nowhere. Each puzzle was solvable on paper from the screenshot. The teaching was clear because the title line states the rule for each level.

Difficulty: levels 1-3 were 1/5. Levels 4-7 were 2/5. Level 6 was a 2.5.
Satisfaction: 4/5 on each of levels 1-7, with level 6 at 5/5.

Would I play on: yes, I would keep going, but only a little. The rules are clean and the hint text is good. I wanted to see the Gates chapter. As a judge I would want more surprise by level 7. It is still a tidy, fair puzzle game.
