AI persona: maya

Exit interview. Casual phone gamer, impatient, skims the text. I played from level 5 onward as asked and got through the whole main game (levels 5 to 12). I did not open Night shift.

**What the game is about**
A small grid puzzler. You are a white circle, and the goal is to get to the EXIT. You can't do it alone: you hop into other bodies (cats and guards, asleep with a z) and walk them around, and each body can do things the others can't. The title and a one-line riddle at the top of each level are the hint. I mostly read the title and the question, so I went by trial and error more than by reading the legend.

**Rules as I understood them**
- You are the white circle and weigh nothing. Standing on a plate does not hold the gate up for you. (Confirmed: level 5 message "You weigh nothing: the plate stays up.")
- Walking into a sleeping body hops you into it. The body you left stays where it was and goes idle. (Confirmed many times.)
- A plate holds the gates open only while a body is on it. (Confirmed, level 5.)
- Gates also open when a body is standing in any gate. (Stated in the level 9 text, and it worked. Confirmed.)
- Vents (grey grille) are cat-only. (Confirmed, level 7 text and play.)
- The padlocked door can only be opened by the guard. (Confirmed, level 6: "Blocked: only a guard can open the locked door.")
- Red striped squares: the cat can walk on them and the guard can't. (Cat walking onto them confirmed at level 6. The guard being blocked by them is a guess.)
- Blue striped squares: the guard can walk on them. (Confirmed at level 11, the guard walked over one. The cat being blocked by them is a guess.)
- Hopping only works to an orthogonally adjacent body, not diagonal. (Guessed. I never tried a diagonal hop, I just planned around it.)
- Turns are counted per move and per hop, and the par number is a target. Z undoes. (Confirmed, though I barely used undo.)

**Best moment**
Level 9, "Two gates". Once I read that a body in a gate counts as holding it open, the whole thing clicked: the guard parks in the lower gate, the cat runs up and through the top gate and the vent. Nine turns, on par. Felt clever for about thirty seconds.

**Worst moment**
Level 6. I misread the grid by a column and walked the cat into the wrong place, then spent a lot of turns going round in circles trying to get back into the guard. The messages helped (the "cat can't see red" note told me the cat could walk the red squares), but I was annoyed at myself. 21 turns against a par of 13.

**Where I got stuck**
- Level 6: how to get back into the guard after the cat moved. Solved it by parking the cat on a red square diagonal from the guard's path, then hopping into the guard from there.
- Level 8: I first thought the guard was boxed in. I had to find the route where the guard hops into the lower cat, which then sits on the plate.
- Level 11: the cats and guard all blocking each other in a corridor. I ended up with the upper cat on a red square in the far left corner by accident, but it was out of the way, so I took it.
- Level 12: the hardest for me. You need the guard off the plate while a cat takes its place, and the cats have to shuffle in a particular order. I got it, but it took a lot of hops.

**Would I play on?**
Maybe, but not right now. I finished the main game and the chapters felt good. The night shift is optional and the last few levels were long, so I'd want shorter puzzles before I'd go on. If there was a level-select or a restart button that made it quicker to re-try, I'd probably come back. I quit after level 12 said "That's the whole main game."

**Confusion**
The thing that confused me most was which squares each body could walk on. The striped red and blue blocks behaved differently for the cat and the guard, and I had to learn it through messages and trial. A short on-screen key would have saved me a few turns.
