# Exit interview: Kenji (puzzle expert), levels 3 to 10

## What the game is about
You are a weightless spirit that hops into sleeping bodies (guard, cat). Each body sees the world differently: the guard sees red, the cat sees blue, the spirit sees no colour. What you can walk on depends on which body you are in. You have to get some body onto the EXIT, often by leaving other bodies behind as tools (paperweights on plates, plugs in gates).

## Rules as I understood them
- Walk into an adjacent body to hop into it. The body you leave stays where it is. (confirmed)
- Hops work from any tile you can stand on, including a doorway or inside a coloured block. (confirmed, levels 4, 6, 8)
- Guard: opens locked doors, walks red bridges, blocked by red blocks, can't use vents. (confirmed via legend and play)
- Cat: walks through red blocks and blue bridges, fits through vents, can't use locked doors. (confirmed)
- Spirit: plain floor, plates, exit, open gates only; weighs nothing on plates. (confirmed via legend; I never tested the weight myself)
- A body on a plate opens all gates. (confirmed, levels 5, 7, 8, 10)
- A body standing in a gate keeps all gates open, not just that one. (confirmed, level 9)
- Sleeping bodies block movement. (from the legend, guessed in practice: I never bumped into one without meaning to hop)
- Any body reaching the exit wins. (confirmed: I won as the guard and as the cat)

## Best moment
Level 8, Bucket brigade. Cat 1 hops into the guard from inside the red block, the guard passes to cat 2, cat 2 walks onto the plate, and then the soul goes back up the same chain to the cat parked in the wall. That is a real "aha", and it uses everything taught so far.

## Worst moment
Level 10's hint. "That guard has one way out: the tile next to it. What will you leave standing on that tile?" My solution left nothing on that tile: fetch the guard, walk it to the plate, hop into the cat. That was par. The hint pointed at an idea I never needed, so either I found an unintended route or the hint is misleading. Also, the hints on 6 and 8 mostly give the answer away. For me, they took the discovery out of the puzzle.

## Where I got stuck
I didn't get stuck anywhere. I worked each level out from the first screenshot and solved it at par on the first attempt. The only time I stopped was on level 4, where I opened the legend to check what the pink tiles were (red blocks).
