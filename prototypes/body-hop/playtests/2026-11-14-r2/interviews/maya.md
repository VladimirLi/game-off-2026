# Exit interview: Maya (casual phone gamer, impatient, skims text)

## What the game is about
Body Hop is a little puzzle game about hopping your white circle into other bodies (guards, cats) and using each body's abilities to get to the exit. I didn't read much of the text, so I mostly learned by poking at things. Each level has a one-line hint at the top, which is the only text I really read.

## Rules as I understood them
- Walk with the arrow keys. Walking into a body hops you into it. (confirmed)
- When you hop, the body you left stays where it was. (confirmed, although it was hard to see)
- Guards open locked doors (orange keys) by walking into them. (confirmed on level 3)
- Cats can cross the blue bridge; to guards and to me it's a pit. (confirmed on level 3)
- Red blocks are walls to the guard but cats walk through them. (confirmed on levels 4 and 8, though the spirit "can't pass a red block" on 8)
- A body standing on a plate (dotted square) holds a gate open. Me, the white circle, doesn't count. (confirmed on 5 and 7, guessed on the details)
- The grey grille is a vent only a cat fits through. (confirmed on level 7)
- Z undoes. R restarts. N goes to the next level. (confirmed)

## Best moment
Level 7. I walked the guard onto the plate, then hopped into the cat and ran it through the gate and vent. Everything clicked and I finished right at par (13). Level 6 was also nice, where I moved the cat out of the way and got back into the guard.

## Worst moment
Level 5. The plate was a fiddly mess. I stepped off the plate by accident, the gate shut, and I kept swapping bodies around without really understanding why. It took 15 turns against a par of 9. Also the "red block is a wall to the guard" thing on level 4 cost me a turn before I figured out the cat could walk through.

## Where I got stuck
Level 8, "Bucket brigade." I could only reach the top half of the board. The red wall between me and the guard is blocked for the spirit, and the bottom half seemed unreachable without a chain of hops I couldn't work out. I tried getting the cat into the red wall and hopping into the guard, but I couldn't see a way for me to end up back as the circle on the top side with something heavy on the plate. The hint ("who can the guard reach without taking a step?") didn't help me. I'm not going to puzzle over it for ages.

I didn't get to levels 9 or 10.

## Difficulty and satisfaction (per level)
- Level 3, "Cat's bridge": difficulty 2 of 5, satisfaction 4 of 5. Simple once I saw the locked door needed a guard.
- Level 4, "Side by side": difficulty 3 of 5, satisfaction 4 of 5. The red walls confused me at first, but it came together.
- Level 5, "Paperweight": difficulty 4 of 5, satisfaction 2 of 5. Too fiddly. The plate rule was unclear and I took too many turns.
- Level 6, "Step aside": difficulty 2 of 5, satisfaction 4 of 5. Clear hint, clean solve at par.
- Level 7, "Who goes first": difficulty 3 of 5, satisfaction 5 of 5. Best one.
- Level 8, "Bucket brigade": difficulty 5 of 5, satisfaction 1 of 5. I got stuck and stopped. Not fun for me.

## Would I play on?
Probably not. I stopped on level 8 after a few tries because I couldn't see the solution and didn't want to spend more time on it. The early levels were fun enough, and level 7 made me want to keep going, but I'm playing on a phone while doing other things. Levels that take a long chain of hops with no obvious path lose me fast. If there were a quicker hint or a way to skip stuck levels without feeling bad, I might come back. As it is, I'm done.
