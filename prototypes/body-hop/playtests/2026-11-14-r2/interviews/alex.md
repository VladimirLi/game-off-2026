AI persona: alex (speedrunner, tries to break things)

What the game is about: You are a spirit that hops into sleeping bodies (cat, guard) to get one of them to the exit. Each body sees different colours and can cross different tiles.

Rules as I understood them
- Walk into a sleeping body to hop into it: confirmed.
- Spirit walks by itself on floor and weighs nothing on plates: confirmed. I only realised the spirit could walk around freely on my second try of X1, and that was the key to the level.
- Plates held by a body open gates: confirmed. I tested a shut gate and got a clear message.
- Guard passes blue blocks, cat does not: partly confirmed from the legend. I did not test it.
- Bodies you leave stay where they are and block the way, and you can only leave one by hopping into an adjacent body: confirmed. This is the main constraint, and it was what hurt me.
- A body standing in a gate also opens it: guessed, from the legend.

Best moment: In X1 I realised the spirit could walk the pocket and hop the guard from the left side, without hopping the cat first. The solve came out at exactly par (25) on the first run of the new plan.

Worst moment: In X1 I spent a long time reasoning about hop order and got nowhere. Then in X2 I could not find a parking spot for the third cat. I started wondering whether the level was solvable at all.

Where I got stuck: X2 "Knot". Three cats and a guard crowd the single corridor. I could not see how to park all the cats off the guard's path while still ending up inside the guard. I quit after about 20 calls and used ShiftN to skip. It worked and took me to X3. I did not play X3.

Difficulty: X1 4, X2 5 (did not solve).
Satisfaction: X1 4, X2 2.

Would I play on? Probably yes, but only because skipping is allowed. I would not grind X2. I wanted a hint that the spirit can walk on its own, and the prompt questions under each title did not give me that.
