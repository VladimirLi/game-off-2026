## ArtCritic

## Art review of the 8 screenshots

**1. Overall cohesion: 6/10**
- **What works:** Each level uses one colour theme (grey, red-brick or blue brick) with a consistent floor tint, so the screens look unified. The characters have clean outlines and read well at this size. The exit, with its green EXIT sign and glowing doorway, can be found instantly.
- **What drags the score down:**
  - **Pixel scale is mixed.** The characters and blocks look like roughly 4× art pixels. The "z" sleep marks, the EXIT lettering, the dotted outlines and the brick mortar are drawn at 1–2× instead.
  - **No shared light direction.** The top wall casts a shadow but the blocks and characters cast none.
  - **The floor is almost featureless.** Large flat squares with a barely visible checker make the rooms look empty.
  - **Low contrast between floor and wall.** In the grey theme (c1, c4, c8) the floor and walls are close in brightness.

**2. Per element**
- **Characters (capped red-shirt kid, blue cat, white ghost):** Charming and readable. The sleep "z" is tiny, a different scale, and spills into neighbouring tiles. The selection outline is 2 px yellow and doubles up with a green square in c5 and c6, which is busy.
- **Walls:** The brick reads well. The dark band under the top wall gives some depth. Every brick is identical, so long runs look like wallpaper.
- **Floor:** Too flat, with no grout lines and no texture.
- **Coloured blocks:** The red diagonal-hatch crate is nice. The blue block with a face is cute but looks like a character, not a block. Same-shaped dotted outlines (red in c3/c7, blue in c2) mark where a coloured block will be. That idea is good, but the 4 px dots are too faint.
- **Grey blocks:** There are three looks — hatched, speckled and a riveted metal plate. I can't tell whether they behave the same or differently.
- **Slabs / open gate:** The green frame with a toothed top edge (c5 (4,3), c6 (5,2)) reads as a spike trap rather than an open gate.
- **Barred gates:** Clear. The red side lights are good.
- **Exit:** Strong, though the sign in the grey theme (c1) crowds against the top-wall shadow.
- **Markers (yellow arrow):** Useful, but it overlaps the sprites next to it.

**3. Top 8 improvements, ranked**
1. **One sprite scale everywhere.** Redraw the "z", EXIT text and dotted outlines on the 4 px grid. Make the "z" 3×3 art pixels and keep it inside the character's tile.
2. **Floor:** Add 1 art-pixel darker grout on the top and left edge of each tile. Keep the checker subtle and add 2–3 random speck pixels per tile.
3. **Drop shadows:** Draw a 1 art-pixel darker strip on the floor below and to the right of every block and character.
4. **Wall/floor contrast:** In the grey theme, darken the walls about 20% or lighten the floor about 10%.
5. **Open gate:** Replace the green frame with spikes with retracted bars — a stub of bars at the top, with an open floor tile below.
6. **Selection:** Use only the yellow outline (no green square) and offset the arrow 4 px outside the tile.
7. **Grey blocks:** Use a single style for blocks that behave the same. Add a 1 art-pixel light top edge and dark bottom edge to all blocks.
8. **Brick variety:** Every 3–5 bricks, make one brick a slightly lighter or darker shade, or chip one corner.

**4. Possible bugs or glitches**
- **c7:** Two identical capped kids appear, at (7,2) and (3,5). The one at (3,5) has a blue square behind it. I don't know whether that is intended.
- **c3 (6,1)–(6,2) and c7 (7,2)–(7,3):** The yellow selection arrow and the "z" draw on top of the character next to them.
- **c1 vs c2:** The same block positions are drawn grey-hatched in c1 but red or dotted in the other themes, so the grey theme hides which colour each block is.
- **c5 (2,1):** The selected kid has both a yellow and a green frame.
- **c6 (7,2):** The exit sits directly against the bricks with no floor tile around it.
