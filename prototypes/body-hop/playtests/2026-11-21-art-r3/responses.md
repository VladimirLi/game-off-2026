## ArtCritic2

**Art review of the 8 screenshots (c1–c8)**

**1. Overall cohesion: 7/10**
- **What works:** the pixel scale stays at about 4 screen px per art pixel throughout, so sprites are about 16×16 on 64 px tiles. Sprites, bricks, floor specks, the EXIT lettering and the "Z" icons all match. Every character has the same black outline. Light mostly comes from the top: blocks have a bright top edge and a dark base, and bricks are lit on their upper edge. Changing the whole palette per level (grey, brick-red, navy) is a good, cheap way to give variety while staying cohesive.
- **What costs points:**
  - Walls have no top cap or edge. Only the top wall casts a shadow strip, so inner wall pieces (for example c1 at (5,2)–(5,3)) look like holes cut in the floor.
  - The floor is flat and has an obvious grid.
  - The UI markers (Z, arrows, rings) crowd the sprites.
  - In the grey levels the coloured blocks lose their colour.

**2. Per element**
- **Characters:** the guard, cat and ghost are charming and read instantly. Closed eyes plus a Z clearly mean "asleep". The yellow ring on the selected character is strong. Weakness: the selected character has no other emphasis, such as an idle bob or a brighter palette.
- **Walls:** the brick texture is clean. They lack depth because there is no cap and no shadow on the floor along the side and bottom edges.
- **Floor:** too empty, with only 1–2 specks per tile and seams that show too much.
- **Coloured blocks:** the raised red block (hatched) and blue block (dotted), each with a dark base, are good. The lowered state, a dashed outline with a faint fill, reads well.
- **Blocks in grey levels:** in c1 and c8 the red blocks turn mud-brown and the blue block turns grey, so they lose their identity.
- **Grey blocks:** the light square with a round boss could be a button, a plate or a crate. It is ambiguous.
- **Slabs:** I can't tell which element is the slab. It may be the grey/lowered tiles.
- **Gates:** the closed bars with red side lights are good. The open state (a green frame) uses the same green as the EXIT sign, so it looks linked to the exit.
- **Exit:** the sign is very legible. The door is undersized, sits low in its tile, and its bright interior casts no light around it.
- **Markers:** the Zs and arrows overlap neighbouring sprites and walls. I don't know what the blue ring around the second guard in c7 means.

**3. Top 6 improvements, highest impact first**
1. **Wall depth:** add a 1-art-pixel lighter top cap on wall tiles that border floor. Also add a 1–2 px dark shadow on floor tiles along *every* wall edge, not just the top one.
2. **Floor texture:** make 3–4 randomly chosen floor variants (cracks, pebbles, worn corners). Bring the seam colour within about 1 shade of the base.
3. **Block colours in every theme:** keep the red and blue hues on blocks in grey levels. Only the walls and floor should change palette.
4. **Marker layering:** draw Zs and arrows on a top layer and keep them inside the owner's tile. Move the Z to the top-right corner, 1 px inside. Shorten arrows so they stop at the tile edge.
5. **Exit:** make the door frame fill the whole tile, with a 2-shade glow spilling onto the floor tile in front. Mount the sign on the wall row above.
6. **Grey block and open gate:** give the grey block a crate bevel, or a clear plate design, so its role is obvious. Change the open gate's green to a distinct colour, or show raised bars.

**4. Possible bugs or glitches**
- c7: the selection arrow under the guard overlaps the guard's legs. The salmon dashed tile marker shows outside the cat's yellow ring.
- c3: the cat's arrow and the guard's Z overlap the guard's hat.
- c1, c6, c8: Zs are drawn over wall bricks.
- c1 and c8: blocks look desaturated or brown. This may be a palette-swap bug.
- c7: an unexplained blue ring around the guard at (3,5).
