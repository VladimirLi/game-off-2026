# Priya exit interview

1. A little ghost possesses sleeping bodies (guard, cat) to reach the exit. Each body sees the world differently, so the level changes depending on who you are.
2. Rules: walk into a body to hop in. Guard sees red (red walls solid, keyholes passable). Cat sees blue (red walls vanish, blue bridges appear) and fits in vents. The spirit sees no colour and weighs nothing. You can hop to an adjacent body even through walls. A body left on a plate holds the gate open. Left-behind bodies block the way. Turns are counted against a par.
3. Best: level 6 (Relay). I planned a four-body chain in my head and hit par first try.
4. Worst: level 5. I miscounted tiles, hopped into the wrong body by accident, and finished at 38 turns versus par 14. Walking into a body hops, so it is easy to do by accident.
5. Stopped after level 7 of 12. Natural stopping point, about 20 minutes in.
6. Red versus blue is hard for me, but the game does not rely on hue alone: red walls are tan/olive and look solid, blue bridges have stripes, and the screen tint changes with the body. Level 2 made it click when the walls vanished as the cat. The keyhole never visibly did anything.
7. Yes, I would keep playing. I would recommend it to cosy-puzzle friends.
8. Gameplay 4, Graphics 3, Audio n/a (heard none) 1, Innovation 4, Theme 5.
9. Change: make accidental hops less easy (show a hop preview or ask before hopping), and show the vision-colour swap more clearly.
