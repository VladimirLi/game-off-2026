# Ruth's exit interview: Body Hop

1. About: You are a ghost (white circle) who borrows sleeping creatures, a guard and a cat, to get each little room's exit. Each creature sees a different colour, and that changes what is solid.
2. Rules as I understood them: Arrows move me. Walking into a sleeping body hops me into it. A guard carries keys and opens keyhole doors by walking onto them. The cat sees blue, so red walls turn to dotted outlines it can walk through, and a blue bridge appears over the gap. Hopping works from adjacent squares, even through walls. A body left on a plate holds the gate open; the ghost weighs nothing. Z undoes. "Par" is probably the target number of moves.
3. Best moment: Level 3. The guard was walled in, and I sent the cat through the walls to hop into him. I felt clever.
4. Worst moment: Not really one. At the start I did not know the circle was me, and I was unsure whether I was meant to hold the key.
5. Stopped: After level 4, by choice. Tired, content. Twelve levels is a lot for one sitting.
6. Confused: The circle, briefly. Also whether a hop costs a turn (it does). The text under each title cleared up most things.
7. Keep playing? Yes. I would recommend it to crossword people.
8. Ratings: Gameplay 4, Graphics 3, Audio 1 (heard nothing, though I cannot tell what I would hear), Innovation 4, Theme 4.
9. Change: Show a small hint on the first screen that the white circle is you.
