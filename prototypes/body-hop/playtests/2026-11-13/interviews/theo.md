# Exit interview: Theo, 12

1. **What's it about?** You're a ghost-thing that jumps into other bodies (guard, cat) to get to the EXIT. Each body sees a different colour.

2. **Rules as I got them (some guesses):**
- Walk into a body to hop into it. Walking off leaves the old body behind.
- Spirit can't walk on coloured tiles. Guard walks on red, cat walks on blue.
- Red-only tiles are see-through to the cat, and blue ones to the guard (guess).
- Guards have keys and the brown locked door opens for them.
- Only the cat fits in the striped vents.
- A body left on a grey plate keeps the gate open. The spirit doesn't count.
- Left-behind bodies block the path, so park them somewhere you can get back to.

3. **Best moment:** Level 2. Hopping the cat and seeing the dashed red tiles just vanish for it. Clicked straight away.

4. **Worst moment:** Level 6. Bodies swap places and I lost track of which one was where. Walked the guard in circles on level 5 too.

5. **Where I stopped:** Level 6 (Relay). Stood on the plate, opened the vent, then swapped out and it shut. I had no idea what to do with the guard blocking the middle.

6. **Confusing bits:** Which body is left where after a hop. The rule's on screen but I didn't read it until level 3 and then it made sense. Level 6 never became clear.

7. **Keep playing?** Probably not past 6. Would tell a friend it's neat for the first five. Not sure I'd call it a must-play.

8. **Ratings (1-5):** Gameplay 4 · Graphics 3 · Audio 2 (I didn't notice any sound, could be muted) · Innovation 4 · Theme (SHIFT) 3. The shift between bodies is the theme, and it's the best part.

9. **One change:** Show what each body can walk on, with a little hint on the tile, and a clearer line about what gets left behind when you hop.
