# Jonas exit interview: Body Hop

1. A ghost possesses sleeping bodies. Each body perceives a different colour, which changes what is solid, so you chain bodies to reach the exit.
2. Rules as I understood them:
- Walk into a sleeping body to hop into it.
- Guard sees red: red walls are solid, the lock opens (guards have keys), and blue things are absent.
- Cat sees blue: red walls vanish, blue bridges appear over pits.
- A body left on a plate holds the gate open.
- Left-behind bodies block corridors.
- The spirit can hop to any body next to it.
- Turns are counted against a par, and Z undoes.
3. Best moment: hopping into the cat in level 2. The whole screen turned blue, red walls became dashed outlines and the bridge appeared. That told me the mechanic without a word. Level 6, with the plate sealed in red walls, was a close second.
4. Worst moment: level 6, where I hopped off the plate cat by accident and had to improvise a fix. A tooltip on what is standing on the plate would help.
5. Stopped at level 7 after about 16 calls, because I was playing for the rating. I had solved 1 to 6, all at par. I would have kept going.
6. Confusion: how hopping and adjacency work (diagonals?), and why the spirit moved so freely in level 5. It was mostly clear by level 5, and the level subtitles carried the teaching.
7. Yes, I would keep playing. I would recommend it to jam people who like puzzles.
8. Ratings: Gameplay 4, Graphics 3 (clean and readable, minimal), Audio 1 (heard none, and I can't verify it), Innovation 4, Theme 4.
   Comment: "Possession puzzle where each body shifts reality. The first four levels teach it perfectly."
9. The one thing I'd change: show the held plate or gate state more clearly, and show what a body can reach.
