# Exit interview: Maya (34, commuter, casual player)

1. **What is it about?** Little guys swapping bodies. I walk into a sleeping thing and I become it. Then I walk it to the exit. Kind of like a sokoban thing, but cute.

2. **Rules, as I understood them:**
- White dot is me, a spirit. Walking into a sleeping body hops me into it.
- Each body only sees some colours. Guard sees red, cat sees blue, spirit sees nothing.
- Tiles that only one colour can see are solid for them and invisible to everyone else.
- Blue striped tiles are bridges, but the spirit sees them as a pit.
- The black gaps are holes. Nobody crosses them, as far as I could tell.
- Keys open locks, maybe. Level 1 the lock just went away when the guard walked in.
- Par is the number of turns to beat it. Going over par is fine, I think.
- Z undoes, R restarts. I didn't work out whether bodies stay behind when you hop.

3. **Best moment:** Level 1. I hopped into the guard, walked it through the lock, and it said "Out in 7 turns (par!)". Felt good, quick.

4. **Worst moment:** Level 2. The cat can't go past the red dashed tile and the guard can't cross the hole. I can't tell if the level is impossible or I'm missing something.

5. **Where I stopped:** Level 2, after about 17 presses and a restart. I was just going in circles.

6. **What confused me:** The colour-visibility thing. It took a few tries to realise the dashed tiles were just invisible to me, not a hazard. Hopping back out of a body and where the old body stays was unclear. Nothing ever said that bodies left behind keep sleeping in place.

7. **Keep playing / recommend?** Not today. Level 1 was fun, but level 2 is a wall. Maybe I'd come back, but I wouldn't push a friend to it. It's not a casual game, it's a brain teaser.

8. **Ratings (1-5):**
- Gameplay: 3
- Graphics: 3 (clean, readable, the colours work)
- Audio: 2 (I didn't notice any sound)
- Innovation: 4 (hopping bodies is a neat idea)
- Theme interpretation (SHIFT): 3 (the colour swap and body swap both count, but it's not loud about it)

9. **One thing I'd change:** Show a hint or a short "try this" the first time a level blocks you. Or let me see what a body can and can't reach before I commit moves. Right now I'm guessing.
