# Alex exit interview, Body Hop

1. **About:** A ghost hops between sleeping bodies (guard, cat) to reach the exit. Each body sees a different colour, so walls and floors change depending on who you are.
2. **Rules:** Walking into a body hops you into it, and the hop costs a turn. Guard sees red: red blocks are walls, red bridges are floor, and blue is absent. The guard has the key for doors. Cat sees blue, same logic, and is the only one that fits vents. The spirit can't pass any colour and weighs nothing. Plates hold gates open while a body stands on them. A body standing in a gate holds every gate open. Parked bodies block the way. Hops only work between orthogonal neighbours. You hop back and forth to relay.
3. **Best moment:** Level 8 (doorstop): finding that the cat in the gate frees the guard. Level 12 was a satisfying relay too.
4. **Worst moment:** Level 11. I assumed the guard could walk through the red block (it's solid for the guard). I also had to learn the gate-body rule from the L legend. It was never taught by a level.
5. **Stopped:** After clearing 12 of 12. Pressing N at the end wraps to level 1, with no ending screen.
6. **Bugs/inconsistencies:** No ending or credits. Level 3's text says "stand next to one, wherever you are", but you still have to walk into the body. Walking in a line accidentally hops you into bodies (I hit the cat in level 3). Failed moves don't cost turns. I never found a way under par.
7. **Targets:** Hit par on all 12 first completion, though a few needed a restart. I never beat par. Pars matched my first solution every time, so I couldn't beat any of them, which felt flat. They did make me plan for exact turn counts.
8. **Ratings:** Gameplay 4, Graphics 3, Audio 1 (heard none), Innovation 4, Theme (SHIFT) 4. Shifting bodies and perspectives fits well.
9. **Change:** Make pars beatable and add a stars or medal tier. Add a final screen. Teach the gate-body rule in a level.
