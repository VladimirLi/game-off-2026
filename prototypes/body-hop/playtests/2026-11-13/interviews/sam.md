# Exit interview: Sam

**1. What is it about?**
You're a spirit that possesses sleeping bodies. Each body sees one colour, and what it sees is real to it while other colours aren't there at all. The puzzle is ferrying control between bodies and leaving the empty ones parked as weights, doorstops or relays.

**2. Rules as I understood them**
- Arrows/WASD move one tile. Walking into an adjacent body hops you into it, and the hop costs 1 turn. (confirmed)
- You can hop body to body from any tile you stand on, including inside a coloured block. (confirmed)
- Spirit: can't pass any colour or stand on bridges ("it's a pit"). Weighs nothing, so plates ignore it. (confirmed)
- Guard: red blocks are walls and blue blocks aren't there for it. Only the guard opens keyhole doors ("only the guard has the key"), and the door vanishes. (confirmed)
- Cat: blue blocks are walls/bridges and red isn't there for it. Only the cat fits through vents. (confirmed)
- A body left on a plate opens the gates and keeps them open. (confirmed)
- A body standing in a gate holds every gate open. (confirmed in L8/L12)
- Parked bodies block movement. Walking into one is a hop, not a push. (confirmed)
- Blocked moves cost no turns and show a message. (confirmed)
- Z undo steps back through hops. (confirmed)
- Arrows next to you mark the bodies you can hop into. (inferred, consistent)
- Whether the spirit counts as a "body" in a gate: guessed no, and I couldn't test it.

**3. Best moment:** L10 "Lay-by". I worked it backwards from the exit, saw the guard had to wait in the blue block as a hub, and got par first try.

**4. Worst moment:** L12. I solved it in 26 against a par of 24. I convinced myself the doorstop cat has to be shuffled out of the gate with two extra hops, and I couldn't find the missing two moves.

**5. Where I stopped:** After L12, the last level. 11 at par and 1 at +2. I spent about 50 actions. The par chase on 12 wasn't worth more time without a new idea.

**6. Unclear or surprising**
- The legend is one dense block that covers every mechanic before you've seen any of them.
- The L9 hint ("what happens to it when you hop in?") didn't match anything I did. I solved it without understanding the hint.
- The arrows next to bodies are never explained.
- A blue block under the guard isn't drawn at all, while other off-colour blocks show as dashed outlines.
- When a door opened there was no message, it just disappeared.
- It's unclear whether you can ever leave a body back to spirit form. I assumed you can't.

**7. Keep playing / recommend?** I'd play more levels, yes. I'd recommend it to puzzle people because it's tight, readable and fair.

**8. Ratings:** Gameplay 4, Graphics 3, Audio 1 (I heard none), Innovation 4, Theme 4 (shifting bodies and shifting perception).

**9. One change:** Teach the legend one line per level, and say what the hop arrows mean.
