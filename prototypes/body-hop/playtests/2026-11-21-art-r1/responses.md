1. **Gate looks the same open or shut.** On levels 5, 7 and 8 I got "The gate opens." but the bars looked closed to me, and after a win the gate looks closed again. I only knew it was open because the cat walked through. The legend's "open gate" icon was the only place I saw an open gate.
2. **I never saw the vent.** Level 7 says "the grey grille under the gate is a vent". The only grille I saw was the gate bars, so I couldn't tell what the vent was. I solved the level without knowing.
3. **Spirit-view blocks were unclear.** In the spirit's grey view, the striped and dotted blocks looked like a crate and a grate. I only learned they were red and blue blocks from the legend (L).
4. **Exit tile wasn't obvious.** I first thought the EXIT sign tile was the exit. The real exit is the lit door below it, so I guessed one tile wrong.
5. **Small white marks on left-behind bodies.** Bodies I had left had small white specks; I took them for glitches or "zzz". The text calls them footprints, but they don't look like footprints.
6. **The guard turned green-tinted.** Seen through the cat's eyes, the guard had green edges, and I wondered if his state had changed.
7. **Hard to see what's under a body.** When a cat stands in a red block or on a plate, the tile underneath is almost hidden. The red outline above the cat was easy to miss.
8. **Diagonal neighbours.** On level 8 the dark shading made a diagonal body look next to me. I walked onto the plate when I meant to hop into the guard.
9. **Yellow corner brackets.** I wasn't sure at first whether these meant "you can hop here" or "selected".

**What looked good**
- The whole level changes colour to show whose eyes you're using (grey for the spirit, red for the guard, blue for the cat). That was clear and fun.
- In the cat's or guard's view, a block it can't see becomes a dashed outline, which is easy to read.
- The legend is excellent.
- The sprites are charming, the "zzz" reads instantly as an empty, sleeping body, and the yellow arrow over the body you control is clear.
- The status text and the turns/par counter are helpful.

**Top 3 suggestions**
1. Draw the open gate very differently: bars raised or gone, with a green light like the legend icon. Keep it open while the plate is held, even after you've walked past.
2. Give the vent its own clear sprite next to the gate, and make footprints look like real footprints or a faint ghost trail.
3. Make it clearer which tiles count as next to you, for example by highlighting hoppable neighbours more strongly. Also show what's under a body, such as a plate edge or block outline peeking out around it.
