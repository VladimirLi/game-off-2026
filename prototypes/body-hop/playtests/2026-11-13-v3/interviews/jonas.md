# Jonas exit interview - Body Hop

1. About: Tiny grid puzzler. You're a weightless spirit hopping between sleeping bodies (guard, cat) to reach an exit. Each body sees one colour, which decides which walls and bridges are solid for it.

2. Rules as I understood them:
- Walk into a body to hop; it must be adjacent, even when it or you stand in a wall.
- Guard sees red (red = walls, red bridges = floor) and is the only one who can open key locks.
- Cat sees blue, and fits through vents.
- Spirit weighs nothing, so it can't press plates, and it can't pass colour.
- Dashed outline means "not solid for you".
- Plates need a body standing on them; a body in any gate holds all gates open.
- Parked bodies block the way.
- The key tile is a lock, not a pickup. I only worked that out from a bounce message in level 4.
- Par is move count.

3. Best moment: Level 8, the chain-hop through the pink wall and guard back up to the cat. Solved it in my head and hit par first try.

4. Worst moment: Level 3 taught me "Guards carry the key" and then the key didn't matter. Also level 4 ran as 11 blind keypresses and I lost track of my body, because the hop turns are invisible in a batch.

5. Stopped: Level 10 (12 minutes in, ten levels, all solved). Jonas stops here because the idea has been shown, but I'd happily rate it.

6. Confused: What the key was (level 4 message). Dark-lined gate meant "vent" (the legend L told me). Level 4 mixed a lock, bridge and wall at once, which was a spike. Mostly clear by level 5.

7. Keep playing? Yes, a bit. Recommend it to puzzle fans.

8. Ratings: Gameplay 4, Graphics 3, Audio n/a (heard none) 2, Innovation 4, Theme 4.
Comment: "Clean little body-swapping puzzler, the hop-from-inside-a-wall trick is great; tell me what keys are sooner."

9. Change: Introduce the lock/key mechanic explicitly in its own early level, and show which body I'm in more clearly.
