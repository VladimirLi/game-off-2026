# Exit interview: Theo, 12

1. **What's it about?** You're a white dot and you walk into other bodies to become them. Guards carry keys, cats see different stuff. Puzzles, I think.

2. **Rules as I understood them (guesses included):**
- Walk into a body and you hop into it. The body you left stays behind.
- Guards are red, cats are blue. Each one sees some colours and not others.
- Dashed things are invisible to the body that can't see that colour, so you walk through them.
- The cat can cross the blue bridge; everyone else falls in the pit.
- Only the guard can pick up the key. The exit needs the key holder, I think.
- Pressure plates need something standing on them to open the gate. Leftover bodies apparently don't count. (Not sure.)
- Par is the turn count to beat.

3. **Best moment:** Level 4 when the guard hopped into the cat and the cat crossed the bridge. It clicked and the chapter finished.

4. **Worst moment:** Level 5. I stood on the plate with a body and the gate stayed shut. I kept hopping and nothing made sense.

5. **Where I stopped and why:** Level 5 "Paperweight". I was bored and couldn't figure out how to hold the plate and walk through the gate at the same time.

6. **What confused me:** The bodies you leave behind. Sometimes they vanish, sometimes they're there. The plate thing. Also whether the key matters on level 3, since the exit worked without it.

7. **Keep playing / tell a friend?** Maybe for a few more levels. I'd show it to a friend if the plate level got sorted, but not as a must-see.

8. **Ratings (1-5):** Gameplay 4, Graphics 3, Audio 1 (I heard nothing), Innovation 4 (swapping bodies is cool), Theme interpretation 3 (the SHIFT part mostly felt like "swap bodies," fine but not obvious).

9. **One thing I'd change:** Show what a leftover body does, right on the screen. Otherwise the plate level is just guessing.
