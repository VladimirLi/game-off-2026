# Exit interview: Sam

1. **What it's about:** You're a spirit that hops between sleeping bodies. Each body sees the world differently, and every body you leave behind becomes a tool: a weight on a plate, a doorstop in a gate, or something in the way. It's a sliding-block puzzle where you are the thing being slid.

2. **Rules:**
- Arrows move one tile. Walking into an adjacent body hops you into it, and that body stays where it is. (confirmed)
- The spirit leaves nothing behind when it hops. Every other body you leave stays parked, asleep, and blocks movement. (confirmed)
- Spirit: can't enter any coloured tile and weighs nothing on plates. (confirmed)
- Guard: red blocks are walls, red bridges are floor. Blue blocks don't exist for him and blue bridges are pits. He holds the key: walking into a door opens it and costs one turn. (confirmed)
- Cat: the same rules with red and blue swapped. (confirmed)
- Walking into a pit, a wall or a locked door is refused with a message and costs no turn. (confirmed)
- A body on a plate opens the gates. A body standing in a gate holds every gate open. You can step from a plate straight into its gate. (confirmed)
- You can hop from inside a wall, a doorway or a bridge tile. (confirmed)
- Vents: only the cat fits. (guessed, never tested)
- Any body reaching the exit wins. (confirmed for cat and guard)
- Undo reverses hops cleanly. (confirmed)

3. **Best moment:** X2 "Knot" and X6 "Go-between". I worked out all 23 or 26 moves on paper, backwards from the exit, and they ran at par on the first try.

4. **Worst moment:** Level 10's hint asks who is left in the guard's "doorway", but the guard wasn't in any doorway, so I spent time looking for a mechanic that wasn't there.

5. **Where I stopped:** After finishing all 12 main levels and all 6 Night shift levels, every one at par. There was nothing left to play.

6. **Unclear feedback or surprises:**
- The level 10 hint, as above.
- When a guard stands in a blue block, the block isn't drawn dashed under him, unlike other "not there" tiles.
- I couldn't tell at a glance whether the darker grey tiles were floor or void.
- The vent icon is never explained on screen. I only matched it to the legend's "vents" by guessing.
- I was surprised that every bump is free. That's welcome, but nothing tells you it's free.

7. **Keep playing / recommend:** Yes. I'd recommend it to puzzle people. The rules are tight and consistent, and the late levels need real planning.

8. **Ratings:** Gameplay 5, Graphics 3, Audio n/a (I heard nothing; 1 if it has to be scored), Innovation 4, Theme 4 (shifting bodies and points of view, and "Shift change" made me smile).

9. **One change:** A small on-screen key for every tile type (vent, void, bridge vs block), so you never have to guess what a tile is.
