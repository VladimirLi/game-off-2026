# Priya exit interview (Body Hop)

1. A body-swapping puzzler: you're a white spirit who hops into sleeping guards and cats, each with different abilities, to reach the exit.
2. Rules as I understood them: walk into an adjacent body to possess it; bodies stay where you leave them. Each body "sees" one colour, and walls of that colour go dashed (passable) for it; the cat's blue bridge is only real for the cat; otherwise it's a pit. The keyhole tile is a locked door only the guard can pass (I learned that from the message "only the guard has the key", not from the icon). Spirit is too light for pressure plates, but a cat or guard left standing on one holds the gate open. You can hop from inside a wall. Undo is Z. Par is a turn target.
3. Best moment: level 5, cat parked on the plate while the guard walks through. That was the "oh!" moment.
4. Worst moment: level 4. My long key sequence went wrong, I couldn't tell why, and the key tile behaving as a door was a surprise.
5. Stopped at level 7, with 6 solved. I'd seen the idea fully and it was getting late.
6. Confusion: what the key does (level 1 it seemed like a pickup). The screen tint per body was neat but the colour names ("sees red") meant nothing to me; I read walls by solid vs dashed instead, which worked. Olive vs blue tint is distinguishable for me.
7. Yes, I'd keep playing and would recommend it to a cosy-puzzle friend.
