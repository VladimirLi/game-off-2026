# Alex exit interview, Body Hop

1. About: you're a bodiless spirit hopping into sleeping bodies (guard, cats) that each see different colours, to get one of them to the exit.

2. Rules as I understood them:
- Walk into an adjacent body to hop. A hop costs a turn. Walls bumped don't seem to cost one.
- Spirit weighs nothing and can't pass colour. Guard sees red (red blocks are walls, blue are not there). Cat sees blue, walks through red, fits through vents.
- Dashed outline means "not there for you".
- Keyhole tile is a door only the guard can pass. It is not a pickup. The exit itself needs no key.
- Plates hold gates open while a body stands on them. Spirit doesn't count.
- A body standing in a gate cell holds every gate open.
- Parked bodies block the way and stay where you left them. Hopping works from inside walls.
- Par is the turn count to beat.

3. Best moment: Level 8, the three-body relay to put a cat on the plate. Also the guard-in-the-gate trick on level 9.

4. Worst moment: Level 3. I misread the grid and bumped my own parked guard twice. Level 5 also stung, I got 11 vs par 9.

5. Stopped: finished all 12 main levels (11 at par, then peeked at Night shift X1, par 25). Poked undo spam, called it done.

6. Bugs/inconsistencies: none found. Spam-undo at turn 0 and wall bumps were harmless. Odd: the keyhole looks like a key pickup but isn't. Hopping out of a body leaves it exactly in place, which easily blocks you by accident.

7. Targets: hit par on 1,2,3,4,6,7,8,9,10,11,12. Missed 5 by 2. Par drove me hard, I wanted that line.

8. Ratings: Gameplay 4, Graphics 3, Audio 1 (heard none), Innovation 4, Theme 4 (shift as perspective swap).

9. Change: make the keyhole read clearly as a door, and show the spirit's weight/hop rules earlier.
