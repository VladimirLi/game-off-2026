# Ruth's exit interview

1. A little white ghost-circle hops into sleeping creatures (a guard, a cat) and borrows their abilities to reach the EXIT.

2. Rules as I understood them: arrow keys move one square per turn. Walking into a creature hops me into it, and the room is tinted that creature's colour. The body I leave stays behind, asleep. The guard "carries the key", so he opens brown lock squares by walking into them. Dashed squares are ghosts to a creature that cannot see that colour (the cat walks through red blocks). Black squares are pits, except the blue bridge, which only the cat can stand on. Par is a target number of turns. Z undoes, R restarts.

3. Best moment: the room turning red when I became the guard, and the pink block turning dashed when I became the cat. That told me what the game was about.

4. Worst moment: on level 3 I hopped into the cat, pressed Down, and walked straight back into my old guard body. I had thought I would move into the cat's square. It cost turns and I felt silly. Also, the lock stayed drawn after I passed it and I never knew why.

5. Stopped after three levels and the cat crossed the bridge. It was a natural place to stop, though I was curious about the rest.

6. Confusing: that the old body stays put and blocks the way (clear after the mistake). Also "par", which I only guessed.

7. Yes, I'd keep playing. It's like a small logic puzzle. I'd recommend it to crossword people.

8. Gameplay 4, Graphics 3, Audio 1 (I heard nothing, or couldn't tell), Innovation 4, Theme 4 (shifting bodies and shifting colours).

9. Change: show where my old body will stay, or give a gentle hint when I walk back into it.
