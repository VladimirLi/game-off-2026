# Exit interview: Maya, Body Hop

1. **What is it about?** A little puzzle where you hop between little bodies (a white dot, a red guard, a blue cat) to get to the yellow exit. Looks like a jam thing.

2. **Rules as I understood them:**
- Walk into a body next to you and you become it.
- Only the guard can carry the key. The orange thing is a lock; bumping it as the cat says "only the guard has the key".
- Walking onto the lock as the guard opens it. It shows up again when the guard steps off.
- Red walls are solid for the guard but the cat walks straight through them.
- The blue bridge is only there for the cat; for everyone else it's a black pit.
- Dashed things are "not there" for whoever I am, so I can walk on them.
- Guess: the exit needs me to be the right body to reach it. Not sure.

3. **Best moment:** Level 1. Guard grabs key, walks to exit, "Out in 8 turns (par!)". Felt clever for about 30 seconds.

4. **Worst moment:** Level 2, I hopped into the cat by accident when I meant to hop back into the guard. Had to walk the whole thing around again. Also the "hop back" rules are fiddly.

5. **Where I stopped:** Level 4 "Side by side". Two guards, a pit column and a cat that can't cross the bridge from where it is. I poked around for a while and didn't see the move. Bored.

6. **What confused me:** Which body I'm in, and where the lock actually lets you through. It clicked in levels 1 to 3, after I learned the pattern. Level 4 never clicked.

7. **Keep playing?** Not past level 4 for me. Maybe to someone who likes puzzles. I'd recommend it to a friend who likes brain teasers, not to my commute crowd.

8. **Ratings:** Gameplay 3, Graphics 4 (clear shapes, colours change to show what each body sees, easy to read), Audio 2 (I heard nothing, so maybe muted), Innovation 4 (swapping bodies and seeing differently is neat), Theme "SHIFT" 3 (the shifting is the core, but it's mostly swapping bodies).

9. **One thing I'd change:** Show the rules once on the first level in plain words, and make hopping back easier. An undo that isn't a key I have to know about.
