AI persona: theo

Exit interview, Theo (12, impulsive, mashes keys)

What the game is about
It's a puzzle game about swapping bodies. You start as a white circle that weighs nothing. You can walk into a body (a red guard, a blue cat, both sleeping) to hop into it, and then you move as that body. The levels are about who stands where, which gates stay open, and who is allowed through which tile. Each level has a one-line hint at the top, and the chapter names are on the pages.

Rules as I understood them
- Walking into a body hops you into it. (confirmed)
- When you hop out of a body, that body stays behind as a leftover in the cell you left. (confirmed, level 6 and 8 footprints)
- Walking alone does not leave anything behind. (confirmed)
- A body on a plate holds the gate open, and the gate shuts when the plate is empty. (confirmed, level 5 and 7)
- A body standing in a gate also keeps the gates open. (confirmed, level 9)
- The grey grille vent is only for the cat. (confirmed, level 7 message and the text on screen)
- The padlocked door can only be opened by the guard. (confirmed, the game said "only a guard can open the locked door")
- Red striped tiles block the guard and the spirit (white circle) but the cat walks on them. (confirmed by messages)
- Blue striped tiles block the cat but the guard walks on them. (guessed from the messages and then confirmed on level 11, the guard reached the exit)
- Red wall tiles are solid for the guard. (confirmed)
- A leftover body on a plate counts as holding it. (confirmed, level 5 and 9)
- Par counts turns, not just moves. (guessed)

Best moment
Level 9, "Two gates". I put the guard into the gate under the plate so he kept everything open, then hopped into the cat and ran it out through the vent. Par on the first try, and it felt like I actually solved it instead of accidentally doing it. Level 11 was similar: I worked out that the cat had to tuck into a side pocket and the guard had to be the one going out, and it went par 13.

Worst moment
Level 6, the cat and the guard swap. I thought I was the cat the whole time but I was still the guard, and I spent about six presses walking him in circles before I realized. Also level 8, I mashed the keys and ended up on the wrong body. That one went 24 turns when par was 16, and my hands were just going.

Where I got stuck
- Level 5 at first: I tried putting the cat on the plate and then walking the cat out, which closed the gate. Took a restart to find the guard swap.
- Level 6: I didn't know the padlock door needed the guard. I bumped it with the cat and it said only a guard can open it. Then I had to walk the cat out of the way.
- Level 8: the wall around the guard's pocket. I couldn't get the guard out and had to use the second cat to hold the plate.
- Level 12: I quit here. The guard is stuck on a plate that's next to a locked door and the gate, and I had no idea which body could reach it without the guard moving. I had about 38 presses in and I was getting bored.

Difficulty and satisfaction (1 easy to 5 hard; 1 bad to 5 great)
- Level 5 Paperweight: difficulty 2, satisfaction 4. Solved at par.
- Level 6 Step aside: difficulty 4, satisfaction 3. Solved at 17 against par 9. The padlock door clue was good but I was confused for a while.
- Level 7 Who goes first: difficulty 2, satisfaction 4. Par 13 on the first real try.
- Level 8 Bucket brigade: difficulty 4, satisfaction 3. Solved at 24 against par 16. Too many bodies in a small space.
- Level 9 Two gates: difficulty 3, satisfaction 5. Par 9. Favourite.
- Level 10 No trace: difficulty 3, satisfaction 4. Par 12.
- Level 11 Lay-by: difficulty 4, satisfaction 4. Par 13 after I figured out the tile colours.
- Level 12 Changing of the guard: not solved, I quit. Difficulty 5, satisfaction 1 so far.

Would I play on?
Yes, probably. The hopping is fun and I like the moment when the trick clicks. But level 12 looked like a lot of boxes and locks, and I would have needed to think more slowly, which I'm bad at. I'd probably come back to it tomorrow instead of right now. The colour tiles were a bit confusing (I couldn't tell which body sees which colour until I read the message), so a clearer hint about that would help a kid like me.
