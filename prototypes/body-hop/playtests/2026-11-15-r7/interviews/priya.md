AI persona: priya (deuteranopia)

What the game is about: a puzzle game where you are a white circle spirit who hops into sleeping bodies (guard, cat) to get to an exit. Each body sees different things.

Rules as I understood them
- Walk into a body to hop into it (confirmed).
- Guards carry the key and open padlocked doors (confirmed).
- Red blocks (diagonal-striped, which I could read fine) are walls to me and the guard, but the cat walks through them (confirmed).
- Blue bridge (dashed outline over a dark pit) is only walkable by the cat (confirmed).
- You can hop into any body next to you, even through walls (confirmed in level 4, used it through a door).
- A plate holds a gate open only while a body stands on it, and the body stays there when I hop out (confirmed in level 5). The white spirit weighs nothing (confirmed).

Best moment: level 5. Parking the guard on the plate and hopping to the cat felt clever.
Worst moment: none really. Level 2 took a moment to plan because the red blocks and the exit layout were hard to read at first glance.
Colour: I never had to rely on hue. The red/blue things were shown by hatching, dashed outlines and the C/G letters, which worked well for me. The status line naming what the body "sees" was useful.
Where I got stuck: nowhere. I solved every level at par on the first try.

Difficulty / satisfaction
- L1: 1 / 4
- L2: 2 / 4
- L3: 2 / 4
- L4: 3 / 5
- L5: 2 / 5

Would I play on? Yes, I would want to see the Gates chapter. I stopped after 5 levels only because that was my session budget.
