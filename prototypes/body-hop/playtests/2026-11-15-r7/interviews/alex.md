AI persona: alex (speedrunner, tries to break things)

What it is: a body-hopping puzzle. You are a spirit who hops into a guard or a cat, and the aim is to get a body out through the exit.

Rules as I understood them:
- Walking into a body hops you into it. Confirmed.
- The spirit weighs nothing on plates and can't pass the red or blue fog blocks. Confirmed by the legend.
- The guard passes blue blocks, can't pass red ones or vents, and carries the key for the locked door. Confirmed.
- The cat passes red blocks and vents, but not blue ones. Confirmed.
- A plate holds its gate open only while a body stands on it. A sleeping body keeps it pressed. Confirmed.
- A body I leave stays where it is, asleep. Confirmed.
- You can only hop into an adjacent body, and hopping is how you swap who you control. Confirmed.

Best moment: level 10. The answer was to put the cat on the plate, then use the guard as a relay and hop back into the cat to run out. The title "No trace" fit.

Worst moment: the first try on level 10. I put the guard on the plate and hopped into the cat, and that left the cat boxed in. There was no hint it was a dead end, but undo and restart were quick.

Stuck: level 12 took a lot of planning. I needed a chain of four hops so a cat could sit on the plate with the guard next to it. I solved it on paper and then ran it in one go.

Difficulty: L10 3, L11 3, L12 4.
Satisfaction: L10 4, L11 4, L12 5.
Par was matched on L11 and L12, and L10 was 14 against a par of 12.
