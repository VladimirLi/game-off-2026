Casual phone gamer, impatient, skims text. I played Body Hop starting at level 5 and went in order.

**What the game is about**
Puzzles on a small grid. I'm a white circle (the spirit) that weighs nothing. I can walk into other bodies (guard, cat) to take them over. Each level has a goal, usually reaching the exit. The text under the title is the hint, and I mostly skipped it until I was stuck.

**Rules as I understood them**
- Walking into a body hops you into it. (Confirmed: the game says "You are the cat now.")
- The body you hop out of stays where it was. For the guard it leaves footprints. (Confirmed on level 5 and 6.)
- The white circle leaves nothing behind when it hops. (Confirmed on level 9, no body appeared where I left.)
- A body standing on a plate holds the gate open. Stepping off closes it. (Confirmed on level 5.)
- Only the cat fits through the vent. (Stated in the level 7 text and legend.)
- Only the guard can open the padlocked door, because it carries the key. (From the legend, confirmed on level 6.)
- The cat can't cross a red bridge. (Guessed from the legend, then the game said "The cat can't see red.")
- The sleeping "z" bodies can still be hopped into. (Confirmed on level 5.)
- Blocked moves seem to still add to the turn counter. (Guessed. The counter went up when I bumped walls.)

**Best moment**
Level 5. The guard stayed on the plate as footprints, and I realised I could run the cat out through the gate. The footprint message was the clue that made it click.

**Worst moment**
Level 8. The room with the guard and the plate looked sealed off, and I couldn't work out how anyone gets in. I wasted a dozen moves and then skipped.

**Where I got stuck**
- Level 6: I didn't understand the striped and dashed red tiles until I opened the legend. Then it was just the guard going around and through the padlock.
- Level 8: I couldn't tell which grey squares were fog walls for the white circle, and the room seemed to have no way in except the red bridge. I skipped it.
- Level 9: I worked out that the body holding the plate has to be the guard, and the cat has to be right beside it when it moves. But every hop left a body behind in the way. I didn't find a sequence that works, so I quit here.

Probably not in this session. Levels 5 to 7 were fun and quick enough. Levels 8 and 9 made me feel like the game was hiding rules, and I'm not patient enough for that. I'd come back if the early levels were a bit shorter to read, and if the fog walls were drawn differently for the white circle.
