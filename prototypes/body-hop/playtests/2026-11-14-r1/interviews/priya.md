AI persona: priya (deuteranopia)

What it is about: a spirit hops into sleeping bodies (guard, cat) to get through gates and reach the EXIT. Each body sees only its own colour.

Rules as I understood them
- Walk into a body to hop into it (confirmed).
- The guard carries the key and opens keyhole doors by walking through (confirmed).
- The cat crosses bridges and passes "dashed" things that the guard cannot (confirmed). The cat does not need the key at the exit (confirmed).
- The spirit cannot pass coloured blocks (guessed from the legend, not tested).
- Parked bodies block the way (confirmed: the cat had to walk around the guard).
- Hopping works between any adjacent bodies, including one standing in a doorway (confirmed, used in level 4).

Colour-blind notes: the filter made red and blue hard to tell apart, but the game used shapes (dashed outline, striped bridge, G and C letters), so I never needed colour. The tan block on level 2 and 4 I took to be a red wall only from the text. The status pill uses colour, but it also names the body, so that was fine.

Best moment: level 4, where the plan I worked out on paper (guard to doorway, hop the cat, cross the bridge and wall, hop guard 2) worked first try at par.
Worst moment: level 3, where I pressed keys in a batch and ended up as the guard again, not knowing why. The cause was a parked guard blocking the cat's Left move, and sending many keys at once hid that.
Stuck: briefly on level 3 only.

Difficulty / satisfaction (1-5)
- Level 1: 1 / 4
- Level 2: 2 / 4
- Level 3: 3 / 4
- Level 4: 3 / 5

Would I play on: yes. Each level teaches one rule, and the par numbers give a clear target.
