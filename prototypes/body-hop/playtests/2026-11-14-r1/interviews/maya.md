AI persona: maya

Playing as Maya, a casual phone gamer who is impatient and skims text.

**What the game is about.** You are a small white circle that can hop into other bodies (guard, cat) by walking into them. Each body has its own rules: the guard carries the key for brown gates, the cat walks through red/pink squares and crosses the blue bridge, the spirit (you, before hopping) sees no colour and can't pass coloured blocks. Each level is a little grid puzzle with a par turn count.

**Rules as I understood them**
- Walking into a body hops you into it. Confirmed (happened every time).
- Guards carry the key and can pass brown gates. Confirmed in levels 1, 4, 5.
- Pink/red squares are solid for the spirit and the guard, walkable by the cat. Confirmed in level 2 (spirit blocked) and level 4 (guard blocked, cat walked over).
- Blue bridge is only for the cat, pits (black) kill others. Confirmed in level 3 (I got a "pit" message on the bridge, which confused me; the cat was still alive and I finished).
- A body left standing on a plate (the square with a dot) holds the gate open, even after you hop out of it. Confirmed in levels 5, 7, 8.
- Hopping out of a body leaves that body where it stood. Confirmed in levels 5, 6, 8.
- Bodies can hop into an adjacent body. Confirmed.
- Guessed: the hint lines at the top of each level are the real rule, so I should read them. I mostly skipped them and still got through.

**Best moment.** Level 5 "Paperweight": parking the cat on the plate and hopping out to the guard so the cat stayed put and held the gate open. It clicked in one go and was satisfying.

**Worst moment.** Level 3 "Cat's bridge": I was standing on the blue bridge and the game printed "pit" in red. I thought I'd died, looked at the screenshot, and was fine. That kind of message is confusing when you're skimming. Also the grid is small and I miscounted columns on the screenshot at least once (level 3 and level 4), so I walked the wrong way.

**Where I got stuck.** Level 4 "Side by side" took me a while. I first tried to take the guard across the red square and it refused ("that block is solid"). Figuring out that the cat had to do the crossing and that red is solid for guards took a few tries. Level 8 "Bucket brigade" also needed some thinking about which body should sit on the plate; I reasoned through it but it took many calls.
