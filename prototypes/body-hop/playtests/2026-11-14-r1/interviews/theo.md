Persona: Theo, 12, impulsive, mashes keys.

**What the game is about.** You are a white circle. You walk into another body and you swap into it ("hop"). Each body has a colour-related ability: guards (red) carry the key and open gates, cats (blue) cross blue-only bridges and walk through red, and the spirit (white) sees no colour. The aim is to get somebody to the yellow EXIT, ideally at par.

**Rules as I understood them**
- Walking into a body hops you into it. The body you came from stays where it was. (Confirmed: happened many times, e.g. level 3 and level 4.)
- Spirit can't pass coloured tiles (pink/red floor). (Confirmed: level 2, "the spirit can't pass colour".)
- Cat walks straight through red tiles. (Confirmed: level 2.)
- Blue bridge tiles (dashed) exist only for the cat; to others they are a pit. (Confirmed: level 3 and level 4.)
- Guard walks into an orange gate and it opens for it. (Confirmed: level 1, level 4.)
- A gate shut by default needs a body standing on a plate (the square with a dot) to stay open. The spirit doesn't count. (Confirmed: level 5, "the gate is shut: weigh down a plate".)
- Cats can pass through open gates. (Confirmed: level 9 and level 10, cat ended on the exit.)
- A body standing in a gate keeps it from closing. (Confirmed by the level 9 text, I did not test it directly.)
- The spirit seems to vanish when you leave it, it does not leave a body behind. (Guessed from level 5, where nothing white was left on the plate after I hopped out.)
- Gates that are open show as dashed. (Guessed.)

**Best moment.** Level 8, Bucket brigade. I had to chain three bodies together: the spirit into a cat, the cat into the guard, the guard into the second cat, and then park that cat on the plate. It came out at exactly par 16 and it felt like a real puzzle, not just running around.

**Worst moment.** Level 5, Paperweight. I didn't understand that the spirit doesn't hold a plate, and my first try ended with me stuck tangled between the cat and the guard. I had to restart and plan it out. I also did a messy hop in level 3 and wandered around for 22 turns.
