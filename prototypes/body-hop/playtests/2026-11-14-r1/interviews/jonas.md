AI persona: jonas (game jam judge, about 15 minutes per game)

**What it is about:** A small puzzle game. You are a white spirit circle who hops into sleeping bodies (guard, cat) to reach the exit. Each body sees different colours, so walls and floors change depending on who you are.

**Rules as I understood them**
- Walking into a body hops you into it. Confirmed.
- The guard carries the key and opens key doors by walking into them. Confirmed (door vanished).
- Red tiles are solid for the guard and the spirit, but the cat walks through them. Confirmed ("the spirit can't pass colour" message).
- Blue bridge tiles exist only for the cat. Confirmed.
- When you hop out of a body, it stays where it was. If you walk back into it you hop back in. Confirmed, the hard way, on level 3.
- Adjacent bodies can hop even when one stands inside a wall or doorway. Confirmed on levels 4 and 6.
- A body parked on a plate holds the gate open. The spirit weighs nothing. Confirmed on level 5.
- Once the key door was opened, it stayed open after I left the guard. Confirmed.

**Best moment:** Level 4. The cat walks into the red wall tile, hops into the guard from inside it, and the guard walks out. That was the first real "oh, nice" moment. Level 6's title and hint set up the same trick and I solved it first go.

**Worst moment:** Level 3. I hopped into the cat, then walked straight back into the guard body I had just left and turned into it again. No warning, and the status label was the only clue. On level 4 I also lost track of which body I was after a long key string.

**Where I got stuck:**
- Level 4. I assumed the spirit could walk through red tiles because the first level 2 used the cat for that. The spirit can't pass colour. The message cleared it up, but only after a wasted attempt.
- Rereading the screenshots to work out which body is which took a while. The bodies left behind look nearly identical to the sleeping ones.

- L1 First hop: 1 / 3. Clear tutorial.
- L2 Dashed means not there: 2 / 4.

I solved levels 1 to 6 and stopped at level 7 because my time was up. It is a tidy ramp, and each level teaches one idea.

**Would I play on?** Yes, a little. The ramp is good and the rules are readable from the level titles. I'd want a clearer visual for "this body is empty and parked" versus "this is me". I'd also like to see whether the later chapters (gates, night shift) keep adding new ideas.
