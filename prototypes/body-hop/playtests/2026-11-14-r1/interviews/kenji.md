# Exit interview: Kenji (puzzle expert)

## What the game is about
You play a spirit with no body. You hop into sleeping creatures (a guard and cats), and each one sees the world differently. A creature only treats its own colour as solid, so the cat walks through red walls and the guard walks through blue ones. Bodies you leave behind stay where you left them. They hold plates down, jam gates open and get in the way. Each level is about choosing who goes where and in what order, so that the right body reaches the exit.

## Rules as I understood them
- Walking into an adjacent body moves you into it, and that counts as one turn. You don't change square. (confirmed)
- The spirit vanishes after its first hop. Bodies you leave stay where they are. (confirmed)
- The guard treats red as solid and blue as open. It's the only one with a key, and doors open for it. (confirmed)
- The cat treats blue as solid and red as open. Blue bridges are floor for the cat and pits for everyone else. (confirmed)
- The spirit can't pass coloured tiles and weighs nothing on plates. (confirmed for plates. Colour blocking the spirit comes from the legend; I never tested it.)
- A body on a plate opens every gate, and so does a body standing inside a gate. (confirmed on level 9)
- Horizontal-bar tiles are vents that only the cat fits through. (confirmed from the legend, then used it)
- You can hop into a neighbour from anywhere: a doorway, a gate, inside a wall. (confirmed)
- Any body that reaches EXIT wins. (confirmed for guard and cat)
- A door closes again after the guard leaves it. (guessed. It still looked like a door afterwards, but I never needed it twice.)

## Best moment
X2 "Knot". It was the first level where I had to plan the end state before moving anything. All three cats had to finish packed into the bottom row. One of them could only take the last spot after the guard had passed it. After that, the "hop out and hop back" chain dropped into place. Getting par first time felt earned. Level 12 was a good warm-up for it: the guard hops into the cat beside it without moving, and that one fact makes the level work.

## Worst moment
Level 7 put a new tile in the vent without explaining it. I had to open the legend to tell the vent bars from the gate bars. Level 10's hint ("What will you leave standing in it?") seemed to point at parking a body in the gate. I just put the guard on the plate and got par, so the hint pushed me toward the wrong idea for a moment.

## Where I got stuck
Nowhere, really. The main game is very gentle. Levels 1-11 were each solvable on sight. Level 12 and X2 needed a minute of planning on paper.

## Would I play on?
Yes. I stopped after X2 only because my session ran out. The Night shift is where the game is for someone like me, and I'd go straight into X3-X6. My advice: the main game could move faster or let experts skip ahead sooner, since most of it is tutorial. Also, if the hints say what to do, they should be optional to show.
