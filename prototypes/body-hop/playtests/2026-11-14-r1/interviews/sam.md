# Exit interview: Sam (methodical engineer)

## What the game is about
You are a weightless spirit (white circle) that hops into other bodies (guard, cat) to get one of them to the exit. Each body sees different colours, so walls and bridges exist for some bodies and not for others. Bodies you leave behind stay where they are and can be used: as weights on plates, as doorstops in gates, or as places to hop to later. Underneath, it's a routing and relay puzzle: who is next to whom, and when.

## Rules as I understood them
- Walk into an adjacent body to hop into it. You become that body and the old one stays put. (confirmed)
- The spirit can't pass coloured blocks ("fog") and weighs nothing. (confirmed, legend)
- Guard sees red: red blocks are walls to it and blue blocks are passable. Guard carries the key for lock doors. (confirmed)
- Cat sees blue: blue blocks are walls and blue bridges are floor. Red blocks are passable. The cat fits through vents (the tile with horizontal bars). (confirmed in play. Which tile is the vent I worked out from the legend text; the legend never draws it.)
- Dashed outline means "not there for the body you're in". (confirmed)
- You can hop between bodies from anywhere, including from inside a wall or a doorway. (confirmed, level 4)
- A body on a plate holds every gate open. A body standing in a gate keeps every gate open. All gates are linked. (confirmed, levels 5 and 9)
- Parked bodies block movement, and walking into one hops you into it instead of moving. (confirmed the hard way, level 3)
- Turn count = successful moves plus hops. Bumping a pit or wall doesn't seem to cost a turn. (guessed: on level 3 the counter looked a little low after some blocked moves)

## Best moment
Level 12 (Changing of the guard). I worked out that the guard steps off, hands me to a cat beside it, that cat hands me to a cat in the red wall, and that one steps onto the plate and hands me straight back to the guard. Planned on paper, hit par exactly, first try. X2 "Knot" felt the same way: a real untangling problem that came out at exactly 23.

## Worst moment
Level 3. After hopping from the guard into the cat, I pressed Down and hopped straight back into the guard I had just left, then kept walking it into pits. Nothing gave me feedback except the badge changing from cat to guard. It's fair once you know the rule, but I'd have liked a clearer "hopped into the guard" message in the middle of a key sequence. I only noticed because the end state was wrong.

## Where I got stuck
I never got truly stuck. Levels 5, 12, X1 and X2 each took a long think before I pressed a key. I also checked the legend on level 7 because of the new horizontal-bar tile. The legend describes vents in words but doesn't show the icon, so I had to infer which tile was the vent.
Minor: level 10's hint ("What will you leave standing in it?") didn't match how I solved it. I just parked the guard on the plate. Either I found an unintended route or the hint is off.

## Would I play on?
Yes. The rules are consistent and fully deterministic, and par is shown up front, so I can plan a whole level before pressing anything. That's exactly how I like to play. I stopped after X2 only because I was out of time. I'd go on to X3 through X6.
