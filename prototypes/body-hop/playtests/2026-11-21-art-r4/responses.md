## ArtCritic3

## Pixel-art review of screenshots c1–c10

### 1. Cohesion and polish: 7/10
**What works:**
- Palette, outline weight and tile scale are consistent across all ten screenshots.
- Swapping the whole room palette (grey, brown, blue) to match the active character is a clever, unified idea.
- The gate lamps, the EXIT sign and the sleeping "z" marks read clearly.

**What holds it back:**
- The floor is low-contrast and samey.
- Characters and their markers often overlap each other.
- Clone characters are hard to tell apart.
- Some states only make sense once you've worked them out, especially the muted blocks in the grey palette.

### 2. Per element
- **Characters (kid, cat, ghost):** Clear silhouettes and charming. The yellow outline plus pointer marks the active one well. **Off:** the copies are near-identical. In c10 the three cats differ only by a 1–2 px collar tint, and the two kids in c7 look the same. The ghost's flat white loses detail against light floors.
- **Walls:** The bricks and the dark shadow strip under top walls give good depth. **Off:** the brick pattern is very busy next to the flat floor, and in the grey theme the inner wall chunks (c1, col 5) blend into the border.
- **Floor:** The tile seams and the little crack and corner details work. **Off:** contrast is too low, the corner tick marks look like stray pixels, and the brown floor (c2) is muddy.
- **Coloured blocks:** The hatched red crates and the riveted blue slab are strong when solid, and the dashed outlines read as "passable". **Off:** the muted versions in c1 look like dirt crates rather than "inactive red", so I couldn't tell whether they're solid.
- **Grey blocks:** A clean metal box with a slot. **Off:** it looks like a switch or safe, and I can't tell it apart from a pressure plate.
- **Plates:** I don't know which tile is the plate. Nothing clearly reads as "step on me".
- **Gates:** Bars with red lamps (closed) and green lamps (open) are excellent.
- **Exit:** The glowing door and green EXIT sign are great.
- **Markers:** The "z", chevron and outline are good, but they collide with neighbouring characters.

### 3. Top 5 pixel improvements, ranked
1. **Make copies distinct:** give each copy a 1 px darker outline, about 60% opacity, or a numbered badge in one corner. Don't rely on collar tints.
2. **Keep markers inside their own tile:** keep the "z", the chevron and the outline inside the character's own 64 px tile. Draw the pointer on top, and drop or offset the "z" when the tile next to it is occupied (c3, c7, c10).
3. **Brighten the floor:** raise contrast between tile fill and seams by about +15% lightness, and replace the corner ticks with a 2 px bevel highlight on the top-left and shadow on the bottom-right.
4. **Give plates their own look:** a recessed square with a dark inner rim and a coloured glyph. Make grey blocks clearly raised, with a highlighted top face and a 4 px drop shadow at the bottom.
5. **Make inactive coloured blocks read as intangible:** keep the hue but use a 50% checker dither with the dashed border, instead of the desaturated brown fill in c1.

### 4. Possible bugs or glitches
- **c6 (3,3):** the kid stands on a black tile with a green lamp on its left. An open gate seems to have drawn no floor under it. In c5 the open gate does show floor.
- **c4, c8 (EXIT under a gate):** the EXIT sign is drawn over the bottom of the gate bars, which looks cluttered.
- **c7 (3,5):** the kid appears inside a blue block frame. It's unclear whether this is intended overlap or a draw-order bug.
- **c3 and c7:** the active character's outline and chevron overlap the kid on the next tile.
- **c10:** the ghost's pointer overlaps the neighbouring cat's "z".

I judged everything at the screenshots' native size. I couldn't zoom in because the image library isn't installed here, so the pixel counts above are estimates.
