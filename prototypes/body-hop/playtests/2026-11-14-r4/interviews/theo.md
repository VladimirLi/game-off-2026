Persona: Theo, 12, impulsive, mashes keys.

**What the game is about.** You are a white circle, a spirit that can hop into bodies. The bodies are a red guard, a blue cat, and some other things. You get each level's exit by walking a body there. Each level has a short line of text on top telling you a small rule, like "Dashed means not there" or "The blue bridge is only there for the cat."

**Rules as I understood them**
- Walk into a body next to you to hop into it. You leave your old body where it stands. (Confirmed: bodies stay put after you hop out.)
- Guards can open the locked brown doors with the key. The cat cannot. (Confirmed: "Blocked: only a guard can open the locked door.")
- Red blocks are walls to the guard and see-through to the cat. (Confirmed on level 2.)
- The blue bridge is only for the cat. (Confirmed on level 3.)
- A plate holds a gate open only while something stands on it. (Confirmed on level 5 and 7.)
- The grey grille vent is only for a cat. (Confirmed on level 7.)
- Gates on level 9 all open and shut together, and a body in a gate holds them all open. (Guessed from the text. I did not get to test the vent part on this level.)

**Best moment.** Level 5 "Paperweight." I worked out that I had to hop into the guard, walk it onto the plate, and then hop from the guard into the cat so the guard would stay on the plate. The gate turned green and I walked the cat right through. I felt like a genius for about a second.

**Worst moment.** Level 9 "Two gates." I was standing on the plate as a cat. I pressed Right and walked off it by accident, and the gate slammed shut. Then I could not work out how to get a body onto the plate and get the cat back out without the mind getting stuck.

**Where I got stuck.** Level 9. I spent a lot of time thinking about hop order and didn't find a route. The mind can only jump between bodies that are next to each other, and every route I tried left the body I needed on the wrong square. I tested one route (plate, vent, left, up into the guard) and it just put me back in the guard with the gate shut. I did not finish it.
