# Exit interview: Kenji (puzzle expert)

## What the game is about
You're a weightless spirit (the white circle) who hops into sleeping bodies (a guard and cats) to get one of them to the exit. Each body sees colours differently, so the same tile is a wall for one body and empty floor for another. When you hop out of a body it stays where it was, so the bodies you leave behind become tools: paperweights, things blocking the way, or stepping stones for your next hop. It's a sokoban-style routing puzzle where the real question is "which body am I in, and who is next to it?"

## Rules as I understood them
- You move by arrow keys. Walking into an adjacent body hops you into it. (confirmed)
- The spirit can't pass coloured blocks or bridges, sees them as fog/pit, and weighs nothing on plates. (confirmed for plates, guessed that fog blocks, since I never tried walking into it)
- Guard sees red: red blocks are walls to it, blue blocks/bridges are not there for it (it walks through blue blocks, falls through blue bridges). Guard carries the key, so it can open brown doors. (confirmed)
- Cat sees blue: the reverse. It walks through red blocks, uses blue bridges, and is stopped by blue blocks. (confirmed)
- Red bridges over pits are guard-only and blue bridges are cat-only. (confirmed in X3)
- Bodies you leave keep their position. Hopping works from any tile, including inside a wall or a doorway. (confirmed)
- A plate holds all gates open while a body stands on it. A body standing in a gate also holds all gates open. (confirmed)
- Vents only let cats through. (confirmed)
- You can't get back out of a body unless another body is next to it. There's no free spirit once you've hopped. (confirmed by planning around it, never tested by trying to leave)

## Best moment
X3 "Junction". The island cat can only leave by its own blue bridge, so it has to run the whole loop back round and come up behind the guard that woke it. Close second: level 12, setting up two cats so one sits beside the guard's step-off tile and the other sits inside the red block next to the plate.

## Worst moment
Levels 1-7 are very gentle for me. I could read the solution straight off the board, and a few hint lines ("Who is already standing where they need to be?") give the answer away. Also, the X1 hint ("After your first hop, who will be next to whom?") didn't match how I solved it. I went to the guard first, so the hint confused me a little more than it helped.

## Where I got stuck
Nowhere for long. X2 "Knot" and X3 "Junction" took real planning: working out the order in which the cats park in the bottom row, and spotting that the guard needs the blue lay-by top-left so the cat can step out and hop back in. I solved every level at par on the first try by planning the full move list ahead.
