AI persona: priya (deuteranopia)

What the game is about: a spirit hops between sleeping bodies (guard, cat) to reach the EXIT. Different bodies see different things.

Rules as I understood them
- Walk into a body to hop into it. (confirmed)
- Guards carry the key and can pass keyholes. (confirmed)
- Red blocks (hatched yellow-grey for me) are walls to the spirit and the guard, but the cat walks through them. (confirmed)
- Blue bridge (dashed outline) is solid only for the cat; a pit to everyone else. (confirmed)
- Bodies next to each other can hop even if one is in a wall or on a plate. (confirmed, used it on level 4)
- A plate holds the gate open only while a body stands on it; the spirit weighs nothing. (confirmed, level 5). Parking a body on the plate and then hopping to another body worked.

Best moment: level 4, hopping the cat while it stood inside a wall cell and then hopping the second guard to reach the keyhole. It felt clever.
Worst moment: level 2 start. I could not tell why the spirit was blocked at the hatched block until I tried it. I also wasn't sure the dark empty cells were pits or just missing floor.
Where I got stuck: nowhere really. The hatched pattern and dashed outline made red and blue readable without colour, which was good for me. Letters G and C on the bodies helped too.

Difficulty / satisfaction

- L2: 2 / 4
- L3: 2 / 4
- L4: 3 / 5
- L5: 3 / 4

Would play on: yes, I'd continue to the gate levels. Stopped after 5 levels because of the task limit.
