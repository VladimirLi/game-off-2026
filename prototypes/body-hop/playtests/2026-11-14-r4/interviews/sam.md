# Exit interview: Sam, methodical engineer

## What the game is about
You are a weightless spirit (white circle) that hops into bodies (a guard and cats) to get one of them to the EXIT. Each body sees different colours, and that changes which tiles exist for it. The body you leave stays where you left it, so the puzzles are about moving bodies into position and passing control along a chain of hops.

## Rules as I understood them
- Walk into an adjacent body to hop into it. The body you leave stays where it was. (confirmed)
- The spirit has no weight and can't carry keys. Once you hop out of it, it's gone. (confirmed for weight; "gone" is guessed, since I never saw the spirit come back)
- Guards carry the key and can walk through keyhole doors. (confirmed)
- The guard sees red, so red blocks are walls to it. It doesn't see blue, so it walks through blue blocks. (confirmed, L2 and L11)
- The cat sees blue, so blue blocks are walls to it and blue bridges are floor. Red blocks are open to it. (confirmed, L2, L3, L11)
- The spirit can't enter red or blue blocks ("fog" or pit). (guessed, I never tried)
- You can hop between bodies even when one of them is standing in a wall or a doorway. (confirmed, L4)
- A plate opens the gates only while a body stands on it. (confirmed)
- All gates open and shut together, and a body standing in any gate holds them all open. (confirmed, L9)
- Only a cat fits through a vent. (taken from the text; I always sent a cat that way, so I never tested it)

## Best moment
Level 8, "Bucket brigade". The hint "who can the guard reach without taking a step?" opened it up. I parked the first cat inside the red block above the guard, hopped down, then sideways into the second cat. When I used that parked cat as the way out at the end, it felt properly engineered. Level 10 came a close second: the spirit weighing nothing meant it could cross the plate to wake the cat.

## Worst moment
Level 12. I had to draw the board out cell by cell to see that once the guard steps up, only one cell touches it. That means one cat waits there and a second cat waits in the red next to the plate. My relay worked but took 21 against a par of 19, and I couldn't find the two-move saving. It wasn't frustrating exactly, but it's the one level where I finished without understanding the intended route.

## Where I got stuck
I didn't get stuck enough to undo anywhere. Level 12 was the only real think: maybe five minutes of planning before my first move, and I still came in over par.
