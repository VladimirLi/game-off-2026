Casual phone gamer, impatient, skims text. This is my exit interview after about 40 tool calls.

**What the game is about.** You play as a white circle that can hop into other bodies (guards, cats) by walking into them. Each body sees a different colour set: the guard sees red, the cat sees blue, and the spirit (me, the circle) sees nothing coloured. Each level is a small grid puzzle with an exit. I mostly just tried things and read the one-line hint at the top of each level.

**Rules as I understood them**
- Walking into a body hops you into it. (confirmed: "You are the guard now", "Back in the cat")
- The body you hop out of stays where it was, asleep. (confirmed in level 6 and level 8: I had to walk around my own leftover cat)
- Red tiles are walls for the guard and passable for the cat. The spirit can't cross red either. (confirmed in level 2)
- Blue bridges are only for the cat, pits for everyone else. (confirmed in level 3)
- Only a guard opens a locked door (the brown key tile). (confirmed in level 4)
- A plate under a body holds a gate open. The spirit weighs nothing, so it doesn't count. (confirmed in level 5 and level 8)
- A grille vent only fits the cat. (confirmed in level 7 message)
- Any body can hop into an adjacent body, even when standing in a wall or doorway. (confirmed from the level 4 hint, guessed for the wall part)
- The spirit can't walk through the key tile. (confirmed: "Blocked: the spirit can't walk through a wall" and then the key blocked me in level 3)
- Par is a turn count. I never really cared about it until the end, which is a mistake I know about.

**Best moment.** Level 8 "Bucket brigade". I finally saw the chain: cat hops guard, guard hops cat, and one cat holds the plate while the other walks through the gate. The screen went green, the gate lit up, and it clicked. Satisfying.

**Worst moment.** Level 6 "Step aside". I bumped my own cat body back onto the guard's path three or four times and lost about 20 turns. The game never told me where the leftover body was. I had to figure it out by staring at the grid. It cost me a lot of time and patience.

**Where I got stuck.** Level 3 (I misread the grid, then didn't realise the key tile blocks the spirit). Level 4 (the black pit column took me a while). Level 6 was the real sink.
