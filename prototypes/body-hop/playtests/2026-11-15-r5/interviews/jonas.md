AI persona: jonas (game jam judge, about 15 minutes per game)

What the game is about: a small grid puzzle. You are a white spirit that hops into bodies (guard, cat) by walking into them. Each body sees different colours, so walls and bridges behave differently per body. The goal is to reach EXIT.

Rules as I understood them
- Walk into a body to hop into it (confirmed).
- Guards carry the key and can pass keyhole gates (confirmed; I never needed to check whether the cat can pass them).
- Red blocks are walls to the guard and to the spirit, but only dashed outlines the cat walks through (confirmed).
- Blue bridge tiles are floor only for the cat, and a pit for everyone else (confirmed).
- Hopping works with any adjacent body, including one standing in a wall or doorway (confirmed, level 4).
- A pressure plate holds a gate open only while a body stands on it, and the spirit weighs nothing (confirmed, level 5).
- Walking into a body you meant to walk past hops you into it. That is easy to trigger by accident (confirmed the hard way).
- Guessed and not confirmed: the par number is a turn target, and going over it is fine.

Best moment: level 4. Hopping guard to cat to a second guard next to the gate, with each body's colour vision making a different route open, felt clever. It was also solved at par on the first try.
Worst moment: level 2. I walked into a body by accident, and it was not clear to me that I had swapped. I lost a few turns undoing.
Where I got stuck: only level 2, briefly. The cause was the accidental re-hop, not a lack of understanding.

Difficulty and satisfaction (1-5)
- Level 1: difficulty 1, satisfaction 3.
- Level 2: difficulty 2, satisfaction 3. Finished over par, 16 against 12.
- Level 3: difficulty 2, satisfaction 4.
- Level 4: difficulty 3, satisfaction 5.
- Level 5: difficulty 2, satisfaction 4.

Would I play on: yes, for a few more levels. The teaching curve is good, with one new idea per level and hint text that matches it. I stopped at 5 levels because of my time budget as a judge, not because I was frustrated.
