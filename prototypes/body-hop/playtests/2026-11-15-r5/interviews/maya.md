AI persona: maya

Maya, casual phone gamer, impatient, skims text. Quitting at about level 9.

**What the game is about.** You move little bodies around a grid to get to the yellow EXIT. You start as the white circle (the spirit). Walking into another body hops you into it, so you take over that body. Guards carry keys and can open the locked doors. Cats are small and can fit through vents and walk over red blocks. The white circle weighs nothing.

**Rules as I understood them:**
- Walk into a body to hop into it. Confirmed (levels 1, 2, 3).
- Bodies you leave behind stay where they are and stay asleep. Confirmed (level 4, level 6).
- Red striped blocks are walls for you and the guard, but the cat walks through them. Confirmed (level 2).
- Blue dashed bridges are only there for the cat. Confirmed (level 3).
- Only a guard can open a locked door (orange tile with a keyhole). Confirmed (level 4 message).
- A plate with a dot holds its gate open only while a body stands on it. Confirmed (level 5 and level 7).
- A vent (grey grille) is for cats only. Confirmed by the level text and the message.
- Hopping works between bodies next to each other, even in a doorway. Confirmed (level 4 text).
- Walking onto a key picks it up. I was not sure about this. On level 3 I walked past a key and it stayed on the board until I stepped back onto it. Guessed, then partly confirmed.
- Gates stay open while a body is standing in one. Level 9 text says so. Guessed how it works in practice, not confirmed.

**Best moment.** Level 5, Paperweight. I parked the guard on the plate, hopped into the cat and walked it straight through the gate. It clicked and I solved it at par. Level 8 also felt good, a long chain of hops that all worked.

**Worst moment.** Level 3, where I walked right past the key and didn't pick it up. I thought it was broken for a second. Also level 4, which took me 25 moves when par was 17, because I kept walking the wrong body into the locked door.

**Where I got stuck.** Level 9, Two gates. The gates close as soon as the body standing in them leaves. I hopped out of the cat in the gate and the gate shut on me ("the gate is shut"). I didn't work out how to keep a body in a gate while another body walks through. At that point I was out of patience and quit.

If the levels were shorter, or if the rule about gates were stated in one line on the board, I might come back. As it is, I'm done for this session.
