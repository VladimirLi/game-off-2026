AI persona: theo

Theo, 12, impulsive, mashes keys. Exit interview after playing through level 9 (quit there).

**What the game is about (as I got it)**
You are a little white circle. You walk into other bodies to "hop" into them and take control of them: guards (red G), cats (blue C). Each body has its own rules about what it can walk through. You move from puzzle to puzzle, get to the yellow EXIT, and press N. The chapters are Eyes, Leftovers, Gates, and the levels get harder as they go.

**Rules as I understood them**
- Walking into a body hops you into it. CONFIRMED. (Level 1, I did it by accident and it was fine.)
- The body you leave behind stays where it was standing. CONFIRMED (level 2, the guard stayed on the square I came from; level 6 the cat stepped aside and I got back into the guard).
- Guards carry the key. GUESSED, and then it looked right: the key vanished from the floor when the guard stepped on it.
- Red walls are solid for the guard and for me, but the cat sees them as dashed outlines and walks through them. CONFIRMED in level 2 and level 4.
- The blue bridge is only there for the cat. CONFIRMED in level 3.
- Locked doors (brown key tiles) can only be opened by a guard. CONFIRMED in level 4 ("Blocked: only a guard can open the locked door").
- Plates hold a gate open only while a body with weight stands on them. CONFIRMED in level 5. The white circle weighs nothing.
- Hopping works through walls and gates too: you can hop into a body standing inside a wall. CONFIRMED in level 3 and level 6 (the cat in the red wall).
- A blocked move ends the rest of the key string. CONFIRMED a few times: the rest of my keys just got thrown away when I bumped something. That was annoying.
- The vent (grey grille) is only for the cat. CONFIRMED in level 7 (I did it right).
- In level 9, a body on the gate or plate holds all gates open. CONFIRMED that the gates shut when nobody is on the plate ("The gates shut: no body on a plate.").

**Best moment**
Level 5, Paperweight. The gate was shut, I was stuck, I restarted, and I worked out that the red guard should stand on the plate while the cat walked through. The gate opened and I got it in 12 turns. I felt like a genius.

Also level 8 (Bucket brigade): I hopped from body to body like a bucket line and hit exactly par 16. It felt like the game was rewarding me for thinking.

**Worst moment**
Level 9 (Two gates). The plate is the only way past and the only thing that holds the gates is somebody standing on it, so I can never be the one who walks through while also holding it. I tried a bunch of hop orders and it kept shutting on me. Then I mashed keys for a while and got nothing. I think it's just impossible for me, or I'm missing something.
