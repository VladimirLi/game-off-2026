# Exit interview: Kenji (puzzle expert), levels 5 to 12

## What the game is about
You play a weightless spirit that hops between sleeping bodies (a red guard and blue cats). Each body sees one colour and can only walk on certain tiles. You leave bodies behind as tools: as weights on plates, wedges in gates, or relay points for getting back into another body later. Getting one body to the EXIT wins the level.

## Rules as I understood them
- Walking into an adjacent body moves the spirit into it. The body you left stays where it is, asleep. (confirmed)
- The spirit can't leave a body except by hopping into another body next to it. (guessed. I never tried, and I never needed to)
- Spirit: walks on floor, plates, open gates and the exit. It weighs nothing on plates. (confirmed, legend)
- Guard: can walk on blue blocks and through the locked door. Can't walk on red blocks, vents or pits. (confirmed, legend and levels 6, 11, 12)
- Cat: can walk on red blocks and through vents. Can't walk on blue blocks or through the locked door. (confirmed, legend and levels 6, 7, 8, 11)
- A plate holds every gate open while a body stands on it. (confirmed)
- A gate can't close on a body standing in it, and all gates open and close together. (confirmed, level 9)
- Any one body reaching the exit wins. The others can stay behind. (confirmed)

## Best moment
Level 12, Changing of the guard. I worked out backwards that the cat on the plate has to hop straight up into the guard, and that meant a second cat had to be parked as a relay beside the guard. That was a nice deduction, and the whole plan worked first try at par 19. Level 8, Bucket brigade, was a close second for the same relay idea.

## Worst moment
Level 10, No trace. It's nearly a corridor with one obvious route, and the hint ("the guard's only way out is the tile you wake it from") didn't change anything I did. It felt like filler between 9 and 11.

## Where I got stuck
I never really got stuck. I solved every level on paper before moving. I opened the legend once on level 6 to check what the pink tiles and the keyhole were. Without the legend I would have had to guess that pink means "red block" and that it's cat-walkable, because red blocks block the guard, who sees red. That colour logic runs backwards from what I'd expect, so the legend is essential.

## Would I play on?
Yes. The main game was easy for me, but level 12 showed the system can do real backward-planning puzzles. I'd go straight to Night shift for the harder levels. If the main game wants to hold experts, levels 7 and 10 could be cut or merged, and the relay idea could show up sooner.
