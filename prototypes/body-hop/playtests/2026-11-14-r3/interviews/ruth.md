I played levels 5 to 12 and finished all eight.

**What the game is about**
You are a white spirit that can hop into sleeping bodies, a guard and a cat. You get one body to the exit by borrowing each body's abilities in turn.

**Rules as I understood them**
- Walking into a sleeping body hops me into it. CONFIRMED.
- The body I leave stays exactly where it was. CONFIRMED. This is the heart of the game.
- A plate holds all the gates open while a body stands on it. The spirit weighs nothing. CONFIRMED.
- A body standing in a gate also keeps all the gates open. CONFIRMED in level 9. I first learned it from the legend and a hint.
- The guard carries the key and opens the keyhole door. CONFIRMED in levels 6 and 12.
- The cat fits through vents and walks over red blocks. CONFIRMED. The guard walks over blue blocks and cannot cross red ones. CONFIRMED.
- Any body reaching the exit seems to count, not only the guard. GUESSED, but it held in every level.

**Best moment**
Level 9. I parked the guard inside the gate so it held the gates open. Then I hopped into the cat, which was standing on the plate, and walked it out. It came out at par and felt clever.

**Worst moment**
Level 5. I stepped the cat off the plate and hopped away, so the cat was no longer on the plate. I wasted moves before I understood that I had to park a body first. Undo helped.

**Where I got stuck**
- Level 12 took the most thinking. I had to work out a chain of hops so that a cat could step on the plate just as the guard stepped off. I did it by pen-and-paper style reasoning, not trial and error.
- I did not know the diagonal-adjacency problem until I thought it through. Hopping only works in a straight line, and that nearly caught me out.
- Reading the legend (L) was essential. Without it I would not have known that red blocks stop the guard and not the cat.
