AI persona: Jonas (game jam judge, about 15 minutes per game)

I started at level 14 (X2. Knot) as the brief asked, with no earlier levels, so I came in cold.

## What the game is about
A puzzle game where you are a small white spirit that hops into sleeping bodies (cats, guards). Each body sees different colours and can walk on different tiles. The goal is to get a body to the EXIT.

## Rules as I understood them
- Walk into a body to hop into it. Confirmed.
- The body you leave stays where it is and becomes an obstacle. Confirmed, and it was the core of the puzzle.
- The guard can cross red bridges, blue blocks and locked doors. Confirmed from the legend and by play.
- The cat can cross blue bridges and vents. Confirmed from the legend; I did not use a vent.
- The spirit is blocked by coloured blocks and bridges. Confirmed from the legend.
- Bodies cannot swap or push each other. Guessed, seems right.
- You can only hop into a body adjacent to you. Confirmed.
- Whether the spirit can ever come back out of a body: guessed, I assumed no.

## Best moment
Seeing the whole screen change colour when I hopped into the guard. The blue block near the exit turned into a plain tile, which told me the rules at once. The first real puzzle, X2, was satisfying once I saw the plan: park the cats in the bottom row, then walk the guard through the blue block to the exit.

## Worst moment
In X3 I walked the cat to the one cell next to the bottom guard and hopped in. The cat then sat in front of the guard and blocked him. I suspect that guard can never leave, and I could not see a way out without undoing.

## Where I got stuck
- X2 took 53 turns against a par of 23. I solved it, but I lost track of which body I was in.
- One multi-key command stopped on a "Blocked: the cat can't walk through a wall" message, and my later keys did nothing. I didn't realise the rest of the sequence was dropped.
- X3 (Junction): I could not work out how to move the bottom guard. I stopped there.

## Difficulty and satisfaction
- X2 Knot: difficulty 3, satisfaction 3. It was fun once understood, but the shuffle of three cats is fiddly.
- X3 Junction: difficulty 5 for a first-timer, satisfaction 2. I was stuck and did not finish it.

## Would I play on?
Probably not in my judging window. Jumping straight into the late levels with no tutorial was rough. As a judge I would want to go back to level 1 and see whether the intro teaches it better. I would rate the idea highly.
