**What the game is about**
Little puzzle levels on a grid. You're a white circle (the spirit) and you can hop into bodies (the cat, the guard) to use them. The goal is to get to the yellow EXIT. Each level has a one-line hint at the top that you're supposed to read, and I mostly did.

**Rules as I understood them**
- Walk into a body to hop into it. (Confirmed: the game says "You are the cat now.")
- When you hop out of a body, a sleeping copy of that body stays on the tile you left. (Confirmed on level 5, when the cat stayed behind asleep. "Leftovers" seems to be about this.)
- The spirit weighs nothing, so a plate only stays down if a body is standing on it. (Confirmed: "You weigh nothing: the plate stays up.")
- Gates open while a plate is held and shut when it isn't. (Confirmed: "The gate shuts: no body on a plate.")
- A body standing in a gate keeps it open. (Guessed from level 9, and it worked.)
- Cats fit through vents, guards carry the key for locked doors, and the pink tiles are cat-only. (Confirmed from the legend. I checked L after levels 6, 10 and 11.)
- The same body can hold a plate while another body walks past it, and the leftover is what makes that possible. (Guessed on level 5, confirmed when it worked.)
